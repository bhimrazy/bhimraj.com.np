## What actually got me to Tier 2

The pattern across my merged work, roughly in order of impact:

1. **Pick one ecosystem, not one repo.** Contributing across LitData, LitServe, LitGPT, and PyTorch Lightning meant maintainers saw the same name everywhere — trust compounds across a team, not just a codebase.
2. **Triage before you code.** Reproducing and narrowing other people's bug reports is undersupplied everywhere and teaches you the codebase faster than feature work.
   > TODO: a concrete example issue you triaged.
3. **Own examples and integrations.** My LitServe deployment examples for vision-language models (Qwen2-VL, Llama 3.2 Vision, Phi-3 Vision) became standalone repos and Lightning Studios — visible artifacts, not just merged diffs.
4. **Show up consistently, not intensely.** Part-time means small, steady PRs beat heroic weekends.

## If you're starting today

- Pick a project you *already use at work* — your bug reports come with context for free.
- Read the project's contributing guide and recent merged PRs before writing code; match the house style ([GitHub's open source guide](https://opensource.guide/how-to-contribute/) is the canonical general reference).
- Make your first PR small enough that reviewing it costs a maintainer under five minutes.
- Keep a public artifact trail — my [OSS page](https://bhimraj.com.np/oss) auto-tracks every merged PR from a daily GitHub snapshot, which turned scattered contributions into a legible track record.
